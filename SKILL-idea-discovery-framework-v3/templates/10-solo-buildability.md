# Template: 10 — Solo-buildability (sidebar)

## Purpose of this section in the framework

Section 10 is a **sidebar**. It judges, briefly, whether **the founder** (as documented in `user-profile.md`) could ship this idea's MVP alone, and what it would take if not. It keeps the judgement calibrated and comparable across ideas, but it **does not influence the decision**.

- **Not part of the verdict.** The verdict comes from the commercial score alone (`SKILL.md`, "Scoring"). There is no gate and no kill here, and nothing in this section triggers a question to the founder.
- **Its output is a short build box** that 13 places under the verdict. It describes *how* a pursued idea would be built (solo, with a collaborator, or with a partner), never *whether*.
- **Never a redesign.** If the founder could not build it alone, the answer is "build with a partner," never "redesign the idea" (`SKILL.md`, "No alternatives after the screen").
- **Skipped** when 09's locked-average early exit applies; the idea is already decided.

Division of labour with 08: 08 asks whether *anyone* could obtain the data, licences and capability; 10 asks whether *this founder* can.

## Inputs

- **05's handoff and 07:** the idea and its form factor.
- **09:** infrastructure the revenue model needs (payments, billing, two-sided onboarding).
- **08:** required partnerships and operational structures, and whether this founder could hold any required licence or partnership.
- **`user-profile.md`:** capabilities, the build-speed baseline (about 20 hours a week) and typical gaps. Read it before Task 2.

## Research activated in this section

Light ordinary web search only, if needed: current tooling for the form factor, realistic build-time benchmarks, what securing a needed partner takes in this market, and infrastructure prices. Treat "I built X in a weekend" posts with suspicion. Deep research is not warranted.

## Analytical work for this section

### Task 1: What is being built

Two lines: the MVP (the version that can credibly test the value proposition), its core technical components, and the infrastructure, data and compliance pieces it needs (from 08 and 09).

### Task 2: Gaps against the profile

A compact table of the capabilities the MVP needs that are **not** already within `user-profile.md`'s documented range. For each gap, show:
- **Gap:** the skill, credential, access or relationship.
- **How it is closed:** learn within the build window / contract it / partner.
- **For a partner:** what kind (technical, domain, data, licensed professional, capital), how accessible it is in this market, and the rough cost to the founder (equity, revenue share, fees).

Close it with one line on local factors that change the picture, if any: talent availability, local infrastructure costs, operating friction, norms around solo founding.

Be honest about the profile's calibration. A mapping that keeps concluding "the founder can learn it" inflates the score. In checkpoint mode, the founder may correct the profile live; note any revision.

### Task 3: Time and capital

At order of magnitude, with conviction labels:
- **Time to MVP:** solo, and with a partner if Task 2 found partner gaps. Include the long tail: testing, deployment, edge cases, security review, integration debugging.
- **Capital for the MVP:** tooling, infrastructure, AI usage, paid services; solo, and with a partner if relevant.

Go-to-market, operating and ongoing compliance costs belong to 11, and multi-year capital to 12.

### Task 4: Score

Score Solo-buildability on the 0–10 scale in `rubric.md`, with an H/M/L conviction label. Apply `rubric.md`'s anchors directly, without paraphrasing; they are tied to `user-profile.md`'s build-speed baseline and learning tiers. Conviction:
- **H:** the profile documents this kind of work.
- **M:** the work is adjacent to documented capability.
- **L:** novel territory for the profile.

Then write the **build box**, the one-line summary 13 will show:

> **Build:** [score]/[conviction]: [solo / solo with significant learning / with a collaborator / with a partner] (~[time to MVP]); [partner needed, if any]. MVP capital ~[range].

## Challenge pass: section-specific questions

- **Time realism:** does the estimate include the long tail of real shipping? A clean, short estimate almost certainly leaves it out.
- **Profile honesty:** was the mapping stretched to "things the founder could probably learn" without a realistic assessment?
