# Template: 04 — Ideation

## Purpose of this section in the framework

Section 04 is the **second diamond's diverge**: the ideation half. It generates ideas that answer the how-might-we (HMW) question in `03-brief.md`. It is the **only place in the framework where ideas are generated**. After 05's screen, the evaluation (06–13) judges the ideas it is handed and never creates new ones. That makes breadth here essential: an idea not generated in 04 will not be considered anywhere.

This section produces:
- A set of **ideas**, each written as a short card with the fields the evaluation will need
- A **coverage table** showing that every variation type was tried
- A **leads check** showing how each of the founder's leads from 00 was turned into an idea
- The section's claims-to-verify list

Section 04 **generates; it does not judge.** It assigns no scores, ranks nothing, drops no idea for being weak, and cannot kill. Judging is 05's job.

## Inputs

- **`03-brief.md`:** the HMW question, the target user and other people involved, the key evidence, the pinned key figures and the open assumptions.
- **`02-define.md`:** the insights and themes, as raw material.
- **`01-discover.md`:** the Actors, Existing solutions and Geography findings, as raw material for the variation types.
- **`00-entry.md`:** the founder's form-factor and market leads, if any.

## Research activated in this section

Ideation works mainly from 01–03. **Light ordinary web search** is allowed to check whether an idea already exists or to find a named comparable (a business that does something similar elsewhere), per the "Name comparables specifically" habit. It needs no confirmation. Do not research markets or economics here; that is 05's and the evaluation's work.

Deep research is not warranted in this section.

## Analytical work for this section

### Task 1: Run the agent brainstorm

Run the brainstorm with agents, each given one **lens** so that they diverge rather than converge. Without agents, run one pass per lens. Every lens answers the HMW question. Give each agent the inputs above.

**Required lenses, one per variation type:**
- **Who pays:** the target user, someone else on their side (an employer, a family member), a third party who benefits, an advertiser, a sponsor, the state.
- **What is sold:** a product, a service, access, a result, data, a tool for someone else to deliver the result.
- **Which side of the market:** serve the target user, or the other people involved in 03 (for example, sell to the businesses that serve the target user rather than to the user).
- **Geography:** where the problem is strongest according to 01, plus the market lead if there is one, plus any market where a comparable already works.
- **Scale:** a small, owned business; a regional business; a venture-scale business. In words only. Each idea's money threshold is set in 05, not here.
- **Form factor:** software (web app, mobile app, browser extension, agent, WhatsApp or chat service, API, embedded widget), a service or concierge, a marketplace, a physical product, or a hybrid.

**Optional lenses, used when they fit the problem:**
- sell to the incumbents instead of competing with them;
- move upstream or downstream in the chain;
- unbundle one piece of an existing solution;
- the desire lens: what would make this enjoyable or delightful, not only less painful.

**Revenue-model prompts** for the "Who pays" and "What is sold" lenses: subscription (per user, per organisation, tiered, usage-based); transactional (commission, take rate, per-transaction fee); marketplace fees on one or both sides; advertising, sponsorship, affiliate or lead generation; licensing (white-label, API, data); services bundled with a product; freemium; selling to intermediaries who pass the cost on (B2B2C); hybrids. These are prompts, not a checklist. Ask, for each idea: who would pay, for what, and at what moment?

### Task 2: Write each idea as a card

Every idea is written as one row of the ideas table, with these fields:
- **Name:** short and distinct. If 05 selects the idea, this becomes its folder name.
- **The idea:** one sentence.
- **For whom:** the target user from 03, or which other person involved.
- **What is sold.**
- **Who pays, for what, and when:** the revenue model in a few words.
- **Form factor.**
- **Market:** the geography.
- **Scale ambition:** small owned / regional / venture, in words.
- **Builds on:** the insights it rests on (by their numbers in 02, e.g., I3), and any named comparable with its citation marker.
- **Relies on:** any of 03's open assumptions the idea depends on (or "none").

**Rules for ideas:**
- **Every idea is an answer to the HMW question.** If a lens produces something that answers a different question, keep it in the table, mark it "outside the HMW", and let 05 decide.
- **Keep every idea.** Do not drop an idea because it looks weak, expensive or unlikely. Only merge true duplicates (the same idea written twice), and say which were merged.
- **No numbers that would be guesses.** No prices, revenues or thresholds. Those come from 05 and the evaluation.

### Task 3: Check coverage

Build the **coverage table**: one row per required variation type, listing the ideas that vary it. Every type must have at least one idea that varies it away from the most obvious answer. If a type has none, run that lens again before moving on. There is **no minimum number of ideas**; breadth across the types is the requirement.

### Task 4: Check the leads

For each lead in 00:
- **Form-factor lead:** at least one idea uses that form factor, shaped to answer the HMW question as well as that form factor allows.
- **Market lead:** at least one idea is set in that market.
- **Founder's idea:** if 00 described an idea rather than a problem (see `SKILL.md`, "How to start"), that idea appears as a card.

List which ideas answer each lead. 05 must then say whether each was selected and, if not, why.

## Section-specific content for the four required parts

The four required parts are mandatory per `SKILL.md`. As a **diverge section**, 04 adapts them:

### Analysis

Five labelled subsections, in order:
1. **Ideas:** the ideas table, with merged duplicates noted below it.
2. **Coverage:** the coverage table.
3. **Leads:** which ideas answer each lead (or "no leads in 00").
4. **Ideas outside the HMW:** any, each with what question it answers instead (or "none").
5. **Claims to verify:** the section's list (for example, "a comparable already does X in market Y").

### Recommendation

Always **"Proceed to 05."** Section 04 cannot kill.

### Rationale

Two or three sentences: how many ideas, whether every variation type and lead is covered, and any lens that produced little (and why).

### Challenge pass

The diverge form, asking **"what did we miss?"**. Each finding uses the fixed short format: what changed and why, or "no change".
- **Clustering:** do most ideas share the same form factor, payer or market? If so, which lens was too weak?
- **Anchoring:** do the ideas stay close to the founder's framing in 00 or the most obvious answer? Is there an idea that would surprise the founder?
- **Disguised duplicates:** are two "different" ideas really the same idea with a different name?
- **Hidden assumptions:** does any idea depend on something uncertain that its "Relies on" field does not name?
- **Facts:** for each load-bearing fact on the claims-to-verify list (for example, that a comparable exists and works as described), is the evidence real and cited?

## Word cap

**Hard maximum (provisional): 1,800 words, excluding citation markers and the source list.** The ideas table carries most of the content; each row stays short. If the cap is reached, shorten the rows, not the number of ideas.

## Downstream dependencies

What later sections consume from section 04:
- **05 (Screen)** judges every idea in the table, selects at most three for full evaluation, flags any others worth evaluating, answers each lead, and sets each selected idea's money threshold from research on comparable businesses at the idea's scale ambition.
- **06–12** start from the selected idea's card. The card's "What is sold" and "Who pays" fields fix the revenue model that **09** evaluates, the "Form factor" field is what **07** judges, and the "Market" field sets the market for every evaluation section. None of these sections generates alternatives to the card.
- **13 (Decision)** lists the flagged ideas from 05. Every idea generated in 04 stays on record in this file for future runs.
